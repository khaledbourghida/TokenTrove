Privacy Policy for TokenTrove

Last updated: July 15, 2025

TokenTrove ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following user information when you register or use the app:

  • Name or Nickname
  • Email Address
  • Free Fire ID

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Track your in-app activity (such as tokens earned)
  • Provide support and improve the app experience

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties except where required by law.

3. Tokens and Rewards

TokenTrove includes a virtual token system where users can earn tokens by watching ads. These tokens:

  • Have no real-world value
  • Cannot be exchanged or purchased in the app
  • Are used only inside the app as a point system

Any external rewards (e.g., Free Fire diamonds) are managed manually outside the app by the admin and are not part of the app’s built-in functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using Firebase (a Google service). We implement standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access or loss.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the app. AdMob may collect non-personal data such as device identifiers and ad interactions to serve relevant ads.

Learn more about Google’s ad privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Children’s Privacy

TokenTrove is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

7. Contact

We are working on adding a contact form in the app. Once available, users will be able to reach us directly for questions or support.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in the app or on our website.
